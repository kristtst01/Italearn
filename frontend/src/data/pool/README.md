# Pool

Content that was good but no longer fits where it was. Nothing here is loaded by the app or read by the content check. Take from it when writing or reworking a chapter.

| Folder | Came from | Why it moved |
|---|---|---|
| `unit-03-things-2026-10/` | The four original lessons of Things (`unit-03`): Masculine & Feminine, The Full Article System, One and Many, Things Around Me | The chapter was rebuilt around the Nouns & articles grammar unit (issue #63). Lessons 1–3 were grammar drills with tips, which the unit's practice now covers. Lesson 4's room vocabulary lives on in the new At Home lesson; its exercises were mostly "What does X mean?" multiple choice. Useful later: the article drills as extra practice, and the *sul tavolo* / *nella camera* sentences for a chapter after prepositions. |
| `unit-10-work-study-free-time-2026-10/` | The seven original lessons of Work, Study & Free Time (`unit-10`) | The chapter was rebuilt as Work & Study, straight after the Regular verbs grammar unit (issue #63). Lessons 1–3 were grammar drills with tips, now covered by the unit's practice. Lesson 4's vocabulary lives on in the new chapter. Useful later: lesson 5 (Free Time & Hobbies) as the starting point for a free time chapter; lesson 6 (More -ere Verbs) for A2, since several of its verbs are beyond A1 (*rendere, intendere, godere, succedere*). |
